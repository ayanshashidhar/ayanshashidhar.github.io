Guided by the core values of the U.S. AFJROTC of "Integrity First, Service Before Self, and Excellence in All We Do," Ayan Shashidhar is committed to the goal of becoming a physician in the armed forces. His fascination for biology and passion to serve his country motivate him. He has competed, and placed, in multiple biology olympiads. He attends the Academy for Careers in Engineering and Science (ACES), a magnet school within Oak Ridge High School (Conroe, TX), and has a 4.0 GPA. He is actively involved in the Air Force Junior ROTC at his school, where he holds multiple leadership positions. He also serves on the ACES Student Leadership Council and Principal's Student Advisory Committee. He earned the Student of the Year award (2024-2025 and 2025-2026) from the Rotary Club of The Woodlands, the President's Gold Award (2024), InnerView Gold Award (2025), and Kroger Gold Award (2026) for community service. He is Executive President of the Echowellness Philanthropy, a student-run organization that raised over $15,000 last year for philanthropic purposes (donating towards a school for mentally challenged children). He is a black belt in Taekwondo and regularly volunteers to teach classes at the Tiger Rock Taekwondo of The Woodlands. He is involved with his local Young Men's Service League (YMSL) and does community service at events in his community, earning the Outstanding Service Award. In addition, he has led a programming camp (teaching Python) at the Hindu Temple of The Woodlands for 4 years as of 2026. He was a finalist in the Houston Brain Bee and Semifinalist of the UK Medicine and Disease Olympiad (both 2026).
